# bifidoAnnotator Example Heatmaps
 
Example heatmaps generated with bifidoAnnotator v2.0 on the AIMS bifidobacterial dataset (49 genomes, 8 species).
 
## GH family and enzyme heatmaps
Available in blue (default) and red color schemes.
 
## Cluster heatmaps
Available in blue and red color schemes, with three row filtering options:
- Default (HMG-utilization = Yes or Unknown)
- HMG-only (--hmg-only)
- All clusters (--all-genes)
